# Opendeck 1.2.0

## New

- **Dark mode with colour inversion.** A header button cycles Auto, Light and Dark (saved in your browser). In dark mode slides are drawn with inverted colours: white backgrounds become near-black and black text becomes near-white, hues are kept and pictures are left alone. *Invert slides in dark mode* (More menu and Settings) turns the inversion off. Printing, copying and PNG export always use the original colours.
- **Mouse-wheel navigation.** Scroll to move between slides, one slide per flick. A slide zoomed in beyond the window scrolls first and changes slides at its edge. `Ctrl` + wheel still zooms. In the slideshow the wheel plays the next or previous build.
